# Instructions  
  A cupcake costs `D` dollars and `C` cents. Calculate the total cost for `N` cupcakes.

  ## Steps
  1. Get three numbers as `input()`: `D` is the dollar cost of a cupcake, `C` is the cent cost of a cupcake, and `N` is the number of cupcakes being ordered.
  2. `print()` two numbers: The total cost in `D` dollars and `C` cents for the cupcakes.

  ## Example
  ### Input
  `10`

  `15`

  `2`

  ### Output
  `20 30`
